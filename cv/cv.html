SchoolLink — attendance and parent notification

GitHub

Platform for secondary schools in Cameroon: teachers record attendance per period on a phone and every absence reaches the guardian the same day by Firebase push or one daily SMS digest. Sole developer: specification, architecture, backend, web dashboard, Android app and deployment.
